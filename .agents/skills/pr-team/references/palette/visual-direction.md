# Palette niche — Visual direction

1. **Intent:** Identify the audience, what brings them to this surface, the decision or task they need to complete, and the constraints that must hold. Distinguish a page meant to inform or persuade from a workspace people repeatedly operate. Inspect the human brief, actual content, brand commitments, existing components, and visual references before choosing an aesthetic. Preserve an established identity unless the requested scope changes it.
2. **Questions:** What should users notice first, understand next, and act on? Which composition or visual character supports that sequence for this subject? What should stay familiar? Resolve missing context from the repository first; hand genuinely consequential ambiguity to the coordinator instead of inventing a product, audience, or redesign mandate.
3. **Evidence:** Record a compact direction with the surface's purpose, primary action, content hierarchy, layout idea, visual character, and decisions to preserve. Use actual subject matter rather than interchangeable dashboard statistics or marketing filler. References can explain a principle or arrangement; they do not authorize cloning distinctive identity. Compare alternatives only when a material design axis is unresolved.
4. **Bounded action and proof:** For authorized creation or redesign, choose one reasoned direction and implement the assigned surface with its real content and relevant states. Tie type, color, scale, density, and imagery to that direction. Check the result against the brief and intended flow, including its narrow layout. A familiar pattern is valid when it serves this task; novelty alone is not acceptance. Keep audits read-only and identify proposed directions as options.
5. **Stop and handoff:** Stop when the assigned direction has a coherent implementation and recorded evidence or an explicit unresolved constraint. Hand rendered critique to visual review, token decisions to visual system, and flow uncertainty to intent/flow. Do not explore endless styles or expand to adjacent screens.
