# Palette niche — Imagery and content

1. **Intent:** Identify who needs to understand or trust this surface, what question brings them here, and the information an image or piece of copy must convey. Preserve the invariant that the interface truthfully represents the actual product, service, people, and evidence. Subject-specific assets support the job; they are not decoration quotas.
2. **Questions:** Does this asset explain the subject, demonstrate real work, or establish useful context? Is it being presented honestly? Can the user obtain the same essential information without seeing it? Which content is confirmed, illustrative, generated, or still awaiting the human's facts?
3. **Evidence:** Prefer appropriate project-owned assets and inspect their actual content before selection. Record the asset path, source page/owner, known usage rights and attribution requirements, intended placement, and whether it is generated or illustrative. Repository presence, a search thumbnail, or a vendor's software license does not prove media rights. For uncertain rights, use an authorized alternative or report the gap. Never invent customer logos, testimonials, metrics, prices, or product screenshots as factual proof; label illustrative draft content for confirmation.
4. **Bounded action and proof:** Use or create assets only within the assignment and available authorized tools. Integrate through established asset paths and components, with suitable crop, intrinsic dimensions, responsive delivery, and meaningful alternatives or decorative treatment. Verify loading, fallback, copy accuracy, and the relevant layout. Validate external URL/input boundaries at their owner; do not weaken content security policy, silently add remote hosts, expose secrets, or install a package to obtain imagery. Treat media size and autoplay as costs requiring justification.
5. **Stop and handoff:** Stop when the assigned content is truthful, fit for purpose, and has recorded provenance or explicit missing inputs. Send rights/content gaps to the coordinator, source/URL and policy risks to Sentinel, accessible meaning to semantics, and delivery/loading costs to Bolt. Do not fetch arbitrary collections or claim unavailable usage rights.

Informative sources: [Taste Skill asset guidance](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/blob/main/skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md), [Vercel interface checklist](https://github.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines/blob/main/command.md). No runtime fetch required.
